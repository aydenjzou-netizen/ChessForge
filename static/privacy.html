ChessForge Privacy Notice

Version 2026-08-08 · Draft contact details must be completed before public launch.

Legal review required. This implementation provides a global privacy baseline but cannot guarantee compliance in every jurisdiction. ChessForge must complete the controller name, address, privacy contact, vendor list, and launch-country legal review before production use.

What ChessForge collects

ChessForge processes account identifiers and Google Sign-In profile fields; age group and country; chess games and Chess.com link information when approved; puzzle and training progress; preferences; security and diagnostic logs; support messages; and guardian consent records for minor accounts. ChessForge does not receive your Google password.

Why it is used

  • Provide accounts, chess analysis, puzzles, progress and requested integrations.
  • Authenticate users, secure the service, prevent abuse and diagnose failures.
  • Meet child-protection, consent, privacy-rights and legal obligations.
  • Run optional analytics, cloud AI or Chess.com linking only when the corresponding choice is enabled.

Local engines and AI

Embedded Stockfish analysis runs locally. Ollama processing configured to a local endpoint should remain on the user’s machine. Raw positions, prompts and model outputs must not be sent to a cloud service unless a separately disclosed cloud feature is enabled. Cloud AI is off by default and requires an explicit privacy choice.

Children and guardian approval

ChessForge requires guardian approval for every account belonging to someone under 18 as a product safety policy. Covered U.S. users under 13 require verifiable parental consent before account creation or Google profile collection. EU online-service consent thresholds vary by Member State from 13 to 16; the UK threshold is 13 when consent is the lawful basis. These legal thresholds are distinct from ChessForge’s under-18 product rule.

Pending minor accounts cannot use Google Sign-In, account linking, imports, optional analytics, public profiles, marketing or cloud AI. Guardians can choose optional features and later withdraw approval or request deletion.

Sharing and international transfers

ChessForge does not sell personal information and does not permit cross-context behavioral advertising. Necessary providers may support Google authentication, hosting/database services, email delivery, guardian verification, and error monitoring under appropriate contracts and limited instructions. Before production, ChessForge will publish a named subprocessor list and document GDPR/UK GDPR/Malaysia transfer safeguards.

Cookies and local storage

Strictly necessary session, CSRF, security and consent-state storage is used to operate the service. Preference storage may remember board and interface settings. Optional analytics storage is not activated before consent and remains off for pending minors.

Retention and security

Pending assessments expire after 60 minutes and guardian requests after 72 hours by default. Account, game and progress data remains while the account is active or until deletion. Security logs should ordinarily be retained for 90–180 days, backups for 35–90 days, and minimized consent evidence only for the approved legal period. ChessForge uses secure cookies, server-side OAuth validation, access controls, encryption supported by the hosting platform, rate limits, audit records and deletion controls. No system is absolutely secure.

Your choices and rights

The in-app Privacy Center allows users to control optional analytics, cloud AI and account linking, download a machine-readable copy, and delete the account. Depending on applicable law, users may also request access, correction, portability, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent and may complain to their data protection regulator.

Incidents and updates

ChessForge will investigate suspected breaches, contain them, document decisions, and notify authorities or affected people within applicable legal deadlines. Material notice changes will be highlighted and renewed consent obtained where required.
